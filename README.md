### Hi, I'm Javier Echube Bestue 👋

**Sociology Undergraduate | Aspiring Social Data Scientist | Mixed-Methods & Cultural Insights Researcher**

I am a Sociology student at the **Universitat de València** (with an international exchange semester at **Eberhard Karls Universität Tübingen**), bridging classical sociological theory with computational methods. My work focuses on decoding social stratification, labor market dynamics, digital culture, and consumption patterns using reproducible research workflows.

I am currently building a mixed-methods research portfolio as I prepare to pursue a Master's degree in **Social Data Science** in Denmark (2028), aiming to specialize in **Cultural Insights** and computational social research.

---

### 🔬 Research Focus & Methodological Approach

* **Quantitative Microdata Analysis:** Modeling socioeconomic stratification, youth labor transitions, digital inequality, and political attitudes using large-scale official survey datasets (INE EPA, INE TIC-H, CIS).
* **Digital Culture & Computational Sociology:** Investigating online communities, algorithmic mediation, and digital discourse across social platforms.
* **Qualitative & Semiotic Inquiry:** Conducting discourse analysis, visual/advertising semiotics, and sociological essays on technology, materiality, and everyday life.
